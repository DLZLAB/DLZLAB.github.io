What is DLZLAB Timing?

DLZLAB Timing is a personal time-management app that runs right in your browser — no account, no ads, no cloud. Plan your week on a visual timetable, schedule daily time blocks, track tasks, habits and goals, and review your statistics. You can install it on your phone or computer like a normal app and it keeps working offline. All your data stays on your own device.

Positive points

Privacy by design

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your schedule, tasks and habits live only in your browser's storage on your device.

Full offline mode

After the first visit, the whole app is cached. Plan your day on a plane, train or without any connection.

Real app feel

Installable on Android, iPhone (add to Home Screen), Windows and Mac — with its own icon, full screen and no browser bar.

Visual weekly timetable

See your whole week at a glance. Tap any slot to add a plan, drag blocks to move them, drag the bottom edge to resize. Overlapping plans are flagged automatically.

Time blocks & daily schedule

A 24-hour timeline for each day. Tap empty space to book a block, move or resize it, and never double-book yourself.

Smart tasks

Priorities, categories, recurring tasks, reminders, due-date planning and instant search (press / on desktop).

Habits & streaks

Build routines one day at a time with a visual tracker and a streak counter that keeps you motivated.

Calendar & goals

Month, week and day views, plus goals broken into milestones with progress tracking.

Statistics

Live, local insights: focus time, completion rate, habit streaks and weekly performance — no data ever leaves your device.

PIN lock

Protect the app with a 4–6 digit PIN. The content stays hidden until you unlock it — perfect on a shared device.

Personalized look

Dark and light themes, accent colors, 12/24-hour time and your choice of week start — all in Settings.

Backup anytime

Export all your data as a JSON file and import it back later — your own insurance policy.

Honest limitations

No cloud sync

Data lives on one device only. If you uninstall the app without exporting first, everything is lost. Use Export regularly.

Forgotten PIN = no recovery

PINs are stored only as encrypted hashes with no backdoor. If you forget it, the only way back is a full reset, which erases the data on that device.

English-only interface

The app itself is available in English only. This guide is bilingual, but the app's menus and buttons are in English.

Notifications need permission

Reminders and alerts only appear after you grant browser permission in Settings → General → Browser Permission.

Single user per device

There are no user accounts. Everyone using the same browser shares one dataset — the PIN only hides it, it does not separate users.

Needs internet for updates

The first load and future app updates require a connection. Over adjusting a cached offline session is impossible.

How to use the app

1

Open & install

Go to dlzlab.com/timetable on your phone or computer. Android: Chrome → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”. iPhone: Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”.

2

Set the PIN (optional)

Settings → Security → Set PIN. Choose 4–6 digits, confirm, then press OK on the lock screen whenever you need to unlock.

3

Plan your week

Open Timetable. Tap any empty slot to add a plan, tap an existing block to edit it, and use the arrows to move between weeks.

4

Plan your day

Open Schedule. Tap empty space on the timeline to book a time block, drag it to move, drag its bottom edge to change the length.

5

Manage tasks

Press the + button (or key N on desktop) and choose Task. Add a priority, category, time and reminder. Tap the check circle to complete.

6

Build habits

Open Habits, create your habit, then check it off daily. Keep the streak alive — the sidebar and Today page show your current run.

7

Set goals

Open Goals, create a goal and break it into milestones. Each completed milestone advances the progress bar.

8

Enable notifications

Settings → General → Browser Permission → Request. Grant it and your reminders will arrive even when the app is closed.

9

Back up your data

Settings → Data → Export to save a JSON backup (keep it somewhere safe). Use Import to restore it, e.g. after reinstalling.

10

Customize

Settings → Appearance: switch dark/light theme, accent color, 12/24-hour format and the day your week starts on.
